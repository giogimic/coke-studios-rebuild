Coke Studios Community & House Rules

Four golden rules every DJ and music fan upholds in our lounges and studios.

🛡 Zero Tolerance Policy

🔒 1. Protect Your Password & Secret

Never share your password, session secret, or red bottle cap PIN codes with anyone. Coke Studios moderators will never ask for your password.

🎧 2. Respect Fellow DJs & Lounges

Keep the vibe positive on stage and on the dance floor. Harassment, hate speech, or spamming chat will result in an instant ban.

⚖ 3. Fair Play in DJ Stage Battles

Decibels are earned through live performance cheers and authentic spectator votes. Automated cheer bots or score manipulation is strictly forbidden.

🤝 4. Safe Trading Verification

Always inspect trade offer slots carefully before clicking Confirm. Authentic trade transfer is final once both DJs lock and accept.
