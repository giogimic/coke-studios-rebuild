Help & FAQs Rules & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I move my character?
A: Click on any floor tile in the studio to walk there.

Q: How do I burn a CD?
A: Open your studio mixer, record loops onto the tracks, and click "Burn CD".

Q: How do I trade items?
A: Click on another player in a private studio and select "Trade".

Safety & Moderation Rules

Treat all players with respect. Never share your password or personal contact details. Moderators actively monitor rooms.
