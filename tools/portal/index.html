Here are my top DJ tips for mastering the game:

  • Earn Decibels (dB): Queue up at any DJ Booth, play your burned CD, and get spectators to Cheer for you!
  • Redeem Bottle Caps: Look under red Coke bottle caps and enter codes in the Red Caps tab for free V-Coins!
  • Decorate Your Studio: Use the Studio Decorator to pick custom wallpapers, parquet floors, and retro chime doorbells.

Coke Studios Safety & House Rules

  • Protect Your Password: Never share your Secret, password, or bottle cap PIN codes with anyone. Coke Studios staff will never ask for your password.
  • Keep It Cool in the Studios: Respect fellow DJs and visitors. Offensive language, harassment, or spamming will lead to an immediate ban.
  • Fair Play in DJ Battles: Decibels are awarded through genuine audience cheer votes. Automated cheering or vote manipulation is forbidden.
  • Safe Trading: Double check all trade offer boxes before pressing Confirm when trading burned CDs and furniture.
