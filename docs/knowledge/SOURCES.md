# Sources and acquisition record

Research date: 2026-10-24. Source types and retrieval states are explicit. The file inventory covers already preserved material; the queue lists additional discovered URLs. Neither is a complete census of everything ever released.

## Primary retrospective notes

**DEV-BLOTTNER:** Mark Blottner describes work on the client UI, moderation, promotional rooms and furniture. His portfolio links examples of the navigator, room scenes, catalogue, dialogs, avatar orientation/hearing and staff tools. It describes occupancy information, stored mixes, inventory and persistent social/economy state. These are first-person retrospective observations; exact builds and numeric rules are not established by the page. The 18 image URLs below were resolved but their pixels were not downloaded.

**DEV-SKOP:** Jeff Skop identifies his product/project-management role across the vendor and in-house platforms, describing an in-house platform in 2004. His product list includes registration, bottle-cap rewards, rooms, furniture, avatars, games and professional moderation. This corroborates scope, not exact layout or reward formulas.

## Retrieval boundaries

The execution environment returned ENOTFOUND for a direct image request. No new original-developer screenshot or Wayback page payload was saved. The collector leaves those entries pending, records the actual failure and stops when the network is unavailable. Web-readable source notes and the fork tree metadata are saved separately from historical payloads.

Live revival pages are classified as current projects. Do not use their modern account flows, multipliers, menus or prizes as original-game evidence. Community wikis supply leads; screenshots with edits, external tools or fan worlds must be filtered individually. No gated content or access controls were bypassed.

Original artwork attribution remains with its creators and Coca-Cola. Preserve repository licenses; a remake code license does not automatically relicense historical art, music or trademarks. See the existing project CREDITS and source manifests for acquisition history.
